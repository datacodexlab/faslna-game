# Product Vision

Create a joyful, understandable mobile game that children can open by link and play immediately. Personal names and a friendly teacher make the experience familiar. Questions develop knowledge; short situations practice cooperation and respectful communication.

Audience: readers aged 6–14, with adult help when needed for the youngest readers. Every child retains independent progress on a shared browser.

Success: a child starts a round without assistance, understands feedback, finishes six cards and can continue after reloading. The website is portable and inexpensive to host.

Excluded: accounts, chats, public rankings, paid services, live AI-generated questions, stores, sensitive-topic scenarios, clinical claims and official curriculum alignment.
